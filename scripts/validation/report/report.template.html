What the hybrid does

ME-rPPG (a 2025 deep model, 2.5 MB, run in the browser) leads. The classic POS colour method reads the same frames and adds its spectrum at a quarter weight; each spectrum also counts by how clean its peak is, so a noisy one is outvoted. Our first design gave POS and CHROM equal weight with the deep model; on 40 people that was worse than the deep model alone, so it was retuned. A window whose fused peak is not clear is refused.

CONTEXT

Why a resting-pulse trend matters in space

Stable at rest in orbit

Holter recordings of seven ISS crew members: night-time heart rate was about 55 bpm both before flight and on the station, while daytime rates were lower in flight.

Fraser, Hughson et al., IAC 2011, IAC-11.A1.2.9

Higher on standing after flight

After spaceflight, heart rate during head-up tilt was 93 bpm against 74 before flight in six astronauts. A stand test on landing day shows the same rise unless compression garments are worn.

Cox et al., J Physiol 2002 (Neurolab); Lee et al., Front Physiol 2020 (11 ISS astronauts)

So: watch the trend, not one number

A resting rate that drifts away from a crew member's own baseline is the signal. The camera reading is compared only with that person's earlier camera readings, and a change is flagged for review only past 8 bpm. It is not a diagnosis.
